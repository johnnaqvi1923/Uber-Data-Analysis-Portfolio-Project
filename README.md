# Uber-Data-Analysis-Portfolio-Project

### 📘 Project Overview

This portfolio project demonstrates a complete **data analysis and visualization solution** built in **Power BI** using Uber ride data.

The project focuses on analyzing the **overall performance of Uber rides across all vehicle types** (Auto, Bike, Go Mini, Go Sedan, Premier Sedan, and Uber XL). It helps uncover key operational, customer, and revenue insights to support **data-driven decision-making** for business growth and efficiency.

---

### 🎯 Objective

The primary goal of this project is to design a **professional Power BI dashboard** that provides Uber’s stakeholders with:

* A clear view of **total bookings, cancellations, and revenue trends**
* **Vehicle-wise performance analysis** across different ride categories
* Insights into **customer and driver ratings** for service quality tracking
* Identification of **top-performing zones and customer segments**

---

### ⚙️ Tools & Technologies Used

* 🧩 **Power BI Desktop** – Data modeling, DAX measures & dashboard creation
* 🧹 **Power Query Editor** – Data cleaning and transformation
* 📊 **Excel / CSV** – Source data preparation and analysis
* 🧮 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – For calculated KPIs and measures

### 📊 Dashboard Pages

#### 🟢 **1. Overview Dashboard**

* Summarizes Uber’s overall ride activity and business performance.
* Displays KPIs for completed rides, revenue, and ratings.
* Shows top pickup and drop locations for better route optimization.

#### 🚗 **2. Vehicle Analysis Dashboard**

* Compares all Uber vehicle categories based on revenue, bookings, and performance.
* Highlights monthly ride trends and contribution percentages by type.

#### 💰 **3. Revenue Insights Dashboard**

* Tracks revenue trends monthly and quarterly.
* Visualizes revenue split by payment method (UPI, Cash, Credit Card, etc.).
* Identifies top revenue-generating customers.

---

### 💡 Insights Discovered

* **Auto and Bike** are the most profitable ride types, contributing nearly **46%** of total revenue.
* **UPI** emerged as the most preferred payment method among users.
* **Customer and Driver ratings** maintain strong satisfaction levels above 4.2.
* **Khandasa** is the top pickup point, while **Ashram** is the most frequent drop location.
